EarthWatch does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not request device-location permission. Coordinates that you type or save, notification preferences, and cached public satellite catalog records are stored locally on your device using the app's database and preferences.

When you use network features, your device contacts third-party services to retrieve map styles and tiles, public orbital elements, or Earth imagery. Those requests can expose your IP address and the map area or imagery date requested to the relevant service provider. The app does not send saved place names or pass-alert preferences to those providers.

The current services are OpenFreeMap and OpenMapTiles for map presentation, OpenStreetMap contributors for map data, CelesTrak for public orbital elements, and NASA GIBS for imagery tiles. Their own privacy terms and service availability apply to requests they receive. The app does not include private service credentials.

Pass predictions are calculated on-device from cached or downloaded public orbit elements. Optional pass notifications are scheduled locally by Android. Saved places and cached orbit data can be removed by deleting individual saved locations or clearing the app's storage/uninstalling it.

The app targets general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The publisher may update this policy when the app or its service providers change. The current policy will be available at [public policy URL]. Contact [privacy contact] with privacy questions.
